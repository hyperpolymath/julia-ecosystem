== Political Instability

=== Political Stress Indicator

The PSI is a composite index combining three destabilizing forces:

[source,math]
----
\text{PSI} = 0.4 \cdot \text{MMP} + 0.4 \cdot \text{EMP} + 0.2 \cdot \text{SFD}
----

where: - *MMP* (Mass Mobilization Potential): Popular immiseration from
wage decline - *EMP* (Elite Mobilization Potential): Elite
overproduction and competition - *SFD* (State Fiscal Distress): Revenue
crisis undermining state capacity

----
