== Secular Cycles

=== Overview

Secular cycles are long-term oscillations (150-300 years) identified by
Turchin and Nefedov in agrarian societies. Each cycle passes through
four phases:

[arabic]
. *Expansion*: Low population pressure, state strengthening, prosperity
. *Stagflation*: Rising pressure, elite overproduction begins
. *Crisis*: Political instability, state breakdown, conflict
. *Depression/Intercycle*: Population decline, elite winnowing, recovery
